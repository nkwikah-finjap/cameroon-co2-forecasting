# Data provenance

Country subset of Our World in Data CO2 dataset, downloaded 2026-10-09.
Source: https://github.com/owid/co2-data
CSV: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/owid/co2-data/master/owid-co2-data.csv
Original emissions source: Global Carbon Project; see upstream codebook for the
specific release, citations and source licenses. OWID compilation is CC BY 4.0,
subject to source-specific attribution/terms.
Variable `co2`: annual CO2 emissions from fossil fuels and industrial processes,
including cement, in million tonnes (Mt CO2). This is not an atmospheric column,
does not include land-use change, and is not a direct measure of health outcomes.
Population and per-capita values are included for context but not used as future
predictors; forecasting them from observed future values would leak information.
Bundled subset contains Cameroon 1960–2024; source raw-file checksum is in manifest.
No invented LSTM/GRU implementation or claim to reconstruct the author's thesis.
